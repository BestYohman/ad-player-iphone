# AD Player for Apple TV - what changed

Each version below is a release on GitHub (Releases, "tv-v1.0.0" and so on). Install with Sideloadly (see README-TV.md).

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-23
- The first Apple TV app: connect to the PC and sign in with the Emby user name and password (once; the Apple TV stays signed in).
- Full and low vision, as on the web page: Home with Continue watching, New with audio description, New TV shows and New movies; Shows, Movies, Search and You. Low vision: bigger text, dark, light or yellow on black, and posters can be turned off.
- A page for each show, movie and episode: the picture fades to black under the names, the show and the season and episode in bold, Resume, Start over, the seasons and their episodes.
- The video plays on the Apple TV with Apple's own player (the Siri Remote's scrubbing), with the audio description first. Its menu has the sound tracks and Stats for nerds. The next episode follows on its own. The Menu button closes it and the place is kept.
