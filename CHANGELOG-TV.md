# AD Player for Apple TV - what changed

Each version below is a release on GitHub (Releases, "tv-v1.0.0" and so on). Install with Sideloadly (see README-TV.md).

## 1.0.1 - 2026-11-23
- **Low or full vision on the Apple TV** is the Apple TV's own choice now: changing it there doesn't change the layout on your phone. The first time, it starts with your account's choice instead of asking.
- **Where you are:** a show's page shows the right Resume and watched marks after the video closes, and Play always picks up from your latest place.
- **When the PC can't be reached,** Home, Shows and Movies say so, with a Try again button (they used to stay empty). Home's rows load at the same time.
- **The remote:** up and down move between rows from anywhere on the screen; a long description can be scrolled to; the player's menu no longer closes by itself while it's open.
- **Fixes:** switching seasons quickly shows the right episodes; a search that's still being typed doesn't show an old error; pressing Stop just after starting a video no longer starts it anyway.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-23
- The first Apple TV app: connect to the PC and sign in with the Emby user name and password (once; the Apple TV stays signed in).
- Full and low vision, as on the web page: Home with Continue watching, New with audio description, New TV shows and New movies; Shows, Movies, Search and Settings (the gear). Low vision: bigger text, dark, light or yellow on black, and posters can be turned off.
- A page for each show, movie and episode: just the big picture (an episode's own), fading to black under the names, with the show and the season and episode in bold, Resume, Start over, the seasons and their episodes.
- The video plays on the Apple TV with Apple's own player (the Siri Remote's scrubbing), with the audio description first. Its menu has the sound tracks and Stats for nerds. The next episode follows on its own. The Menu button closes it and the place is kept.
