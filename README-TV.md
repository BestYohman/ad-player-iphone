# AD Player for Apple TV

The Apple TV app is built by GitHub (Actions, "Build the Apple TV app") from the same code as the iPhone app:
the PC, sign-in and video parts are shared (`ADPlayer/`), the screens are its own (`ADPlayerTV/`).

## Installing on the Apple TV

1. On the Apple TV: Settings > Remotes and Devices > Remote App and Devices, and stay on that screen.
2. On the PC: download `ADPlayerTV.ipa` from Releases ("AD Player for Apple TV").
3. Open Sideloadly. The Apple TV shows in its device list (the PC and the Apple TV on the same network).
   Drag `ADPlayerTV.ipa` in, type your Apple ID, press Start, then type the code the TV shows.
4. Open AD Player on the Apple TV, type the PC's address (the same one the phone uses), and sign in.

With a free Apple ID the app has to be installed again every 7 days (a paid developer account: once a year).
