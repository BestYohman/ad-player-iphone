# AD Player for iPhone - what changed

Each version below is a release on GitHub (Releases, "v1.5.3" and so on). The newest is also "AD Player (latest)".
Install with Sideloadly.

## 1.10.0 - 2026-11-23
- **How do you see the screen?** asked at the first sign-in, as on the web page and Android (needs the PC's app 2.31.0). No vision keeps the VoiceOver layout as it was. Low and full vision get the poster layout: Home with Continue watching, New with audio description, New TV shows and New movies; Shows, Movies, Books and You tabs; a page for each show and movie. Low vision: bigger text, dark, light or yellow on black, and posters can be turned off.
- **The video on the phone** (low and full vision): with the audio description first, a choice of sound track, AirPlay, and Stats for nerds (the picture, dropped frames, the connection, and from the PC whether it's copied or remade and why).
- **Play together:** choose who to invite. Invitations come with Join and Not now (and a notification while something plays in the background). A line says what's going on, with who's in it and what each person's phone is doing, and What happened.
- **Your TV on your phone:** while the TV app plays something signed in as you, "Your TV is playing ... - Join" carries it on here, in step with the TV (needs the PC's app 2.45.0).
- **Play on:** the ones used last come first; Chromecast and Apple TV are listed (an Apple TV that asks is paired once with its code).

## 1.9.0 - 2026-11-19
- VoiceOver: each episode, movie, book and download is one stop. Play together, Start over, Download, Favorites, Ask for audio description and Remove are actions - swipe up or down. The buttons are still on the screen.
- Magic Tap (two-finger double tap) plays and pauses, on the phone, together or on the TV.
- The escape gesture (two-finger scrub) closes Now playing, Settings, Sort and filter, and Chapters.
- Now playing: swipe up or down on the place to go forward or back 30 seconds; it reads "12 minutes of 45 minutes".
- Each part of Home and the lists is a heading, for the rotor.
- Messages wait for VoiceOver to finish speaking instead of cutting it off.
- Sign out and Remove a download ask first.
- When the PC can't be reached, Shows / Movies in progress says so instead of "Nothing in progress".
- TV Play / Pause changes straight away, so a second press doesn't send the same again.
- Lists load once, with the saved sort, and aren't loaded again when going back (unless they're 5 minutes old, or pulled down).
- Large text: the buttons under a row go one under another when they don't fit.
- Siri and Shortcuts: "Play in AD Player" (Siri asks what - a show plays its next episode), "Resume AD Player", "Where am I in AD Player".
- Headphone buttons, the car and the lock screen: next and previous are forward and back 30 seconds. Play with nothing loaded carries on with the last thing played.
- Headphones out or Bluetooth lost: it pauses (for everyone when together). Calls and Siri: it pauses and keeps the place, and carries on afterwards only if it was playing.
- Her place is kept whenever it pauses and when the app closes, and sent to the PC when there's a connection.
- Play together: Stop for everyone asks first; nothing plays until the PC says where; it joins again by itself after a drop ("Back with the others"); swiping the app away while paused leaves the room.
- "No audio description - playing the soundtrack" when an item has none.
- Safer connections: at home the PC's own address is only used once it proves it's this PC; https everywhere else; no cookies; the sign-in kept on this phone only.
- Downloads carry on after a stop, check there's room on the phone, and can be set to Wi-Fi only (Settings).
- Sign-outs are sent to the PC later when there's no connection.

## 1.8.0 - 2026-11-06
- Downloads: a Download button beside each episode, movie and audiobook keeps it on the phone, to play with no connection. For episodes and movies it keeps the audio description (or the soundtrack when there is none).
- Downloads carry on in the background, even with the app closed, and pick up again when the app opens.
- Home has Downloads: what's on the phone and how far each one has got; tap one to play it, or Remove it.
- A downloaded one plays from the phone even when it's chosen from a list, and its place is sent to the PC once there's a connection.

## 1.7.0 - 2026-11-06
- A Play together button beside each Play (episodes, movies, books) instead of the Play together switch. With a TV chosen, Play together moves to the phone by itself.
- The lists are called Shows / Movies with description and Shows / Movies without description.
- Sort and filter on the shows and movies lists: sort by, order, watched, favorites only, genre, parental rating, year. Kept for each list.
- Home at the bottom of every screen, to go straight back to the start.
- Fixed: an episode or movie without audio description closed instead of playing - it plays its soundtrack now.

## 1.6.0 - 2026-11-06
- Home has the same layout as the AD Player web page: TV (Play on, Refresh the TV list), then Play - Audiobooks, Shows or Movies - and that one's search and lists.
- Shows and Movies: Search, All shows / All movies, Favorites, Shows / Movies without audio description, Requests for audio description, and the ones in progress.
- Audiobooks: Search, All books, Series, Authors, and Continue listening.
- Add to favorites, and Ask for audio description for ones without it - as on the web page.
- A "You" part at the end: who is signed in, Settings, Sign out.
